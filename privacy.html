Last updated: August 26, 2026

Remote Jobs Harvest by Trin Millenia ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

This is a simple, free job board. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or payment information on this site. We may also use privacy friendly analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages are popular.

Cookies

This site uses little to no cookies. Any analytics or hosting services we use may set basic cookies to keep the site running smoothly. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

The job listings and some buttons on this site link to third party websites, such as company career pages, application portals, and partner offers. Once you leave this site, we are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults seeking employment and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
